Project 4 · Preference elicitation

How should a recommender ask what you like?

A movie recommender has to learn a new user's taste from a handful of interactions. This project compares two ways of asking: pick one of two films, or rank ten. What we set out to produce is a rigorous study design and the interface that would run it. The study itself is not conducted.

The report covers the feature representation and the extended preference model (Tasks 1 and 2), together with the full design of the user study (Task 3).
