What this was actually about

We are comparing two ways of eliciting someone's taste in films. One asks for a single choice between two options; the other asks for a full ranking of ten. A ranking carries far more information per trial, but it also takes longer and asks more of you. The question is which is the better use of a participant's time — which is why we measured how long each answer took as well as what it was.
