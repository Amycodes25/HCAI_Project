Task 1 — Feature representation

Choose a representation for a movie recommender, justify it, and write the method that extracts it from the IMDB 5000 Movie Dataset.

Why this is the whole model

Utility is defined as U(x) = wTx. That makes the feature design load-bearing in two directions at once. Anything not expressible as a linear function of x cannot be represented at all — "likes Wes Anderson films" only exists if something in x carries it. And w has to be identified from a handful of interactions, so a long vector is not estimable no matter how expressive it is. The interesting work is choosing between those.

Open decisions

  • Genre is multi-label, so multi-hot rather than one-hot — a film can be both.
  • Director and cast are high-cardinality. Frequency buckets, a numeric standing derived from their other films, or dropped entirely?
  • Year as a decade bucket or a scaled number.
  • What dimensionality is actually identifiable from ten comparisons.
