Before you begin

Please read this before taking part. Nothing is recorded until you agree.

What the study is about

We are comparing two ways of asking someone about their taste in films. You will use both, in two short blocks.

What you will do

In one block you choose between two films. In the other you put ten films in order. It takes around ten minutes in total, with a break in between.

What is recorded

  • Your answers, and how long each one took.
  • Your responses to a short questionnaire at the end.
  • A random identifier, so answers from one sitting can be grouped together.

We do not record your name, your email address, or anything else that could identify you.

Your rights

  • Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any point by closing the page, with no consequences and no need to give a reason.
  • You can ask for your data to be deleted afterwards, using the identifier shown on the final page.
  • The data is used only for this coursework and is not shared.
