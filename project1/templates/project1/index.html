What you chose, and what we chose for you

The brief asks who should be in control of each step. This is where that line was drawn, and why.

Dataset
You
Any CSV, with the label in the last column.
Target column
You
Suggested from the column name, but yours to override.
Train/test split
You
The trade-off between fitting and measuring is a judgement, not a default.
Model family
You
The families differ in what they can express and how readable they are.
Score
You
Accuracy and F1 disagree on imbalanced data, and only you know which mistake costs more.
Hyperparameter range
Us
Fixed per family. Choosing a range well needs knowledge of the model that the interface should not demand.
Preprocessing
Us
Median imputation, scaling, one-hot encoding. Mechanical given the column types.
Identifier columns
Us
Detected and dropped, then listed above so the decision stays visible.
Problem type
Us
Classification only. Regression is not supported, and the app says so rather than guessing.
