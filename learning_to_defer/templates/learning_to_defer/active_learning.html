Random querying

Random sampling represents the natural data distribution and generally provides better probability calibration.

Active querying

Active learning prioritises cases close to the deferral boundary, where choosing between AI and expert is difficult.

The trade-off

Active learning improves decision efficiency, but its selected data can produce slightly less calibrated competence probabilities.
