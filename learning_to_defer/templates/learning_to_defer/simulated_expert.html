Why is the expert imperfect?

A perfect expert would make every deferral decision obvious. Real experts also have strengths and limitations.

Why class-dependent competence?

The expert performs differently across news categories. It is strongest for Business and weakest for Sports.

Why deterministic decisions?

The same article always receives the same simulated response. This makes later comparisons fair and reproducible.
