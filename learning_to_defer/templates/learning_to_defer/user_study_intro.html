News Classification Study

Thank you for taking part. In this study, you will read short news articles and decide which topic best describes each article.

What will you do?

You will classify 10 news articles. For every article, choose the category that you believe best represents its main topic.

The study should take approximately 5–7 minutes.

Available categories

  • World
    International affairs, politics, conflicts and major global events.
  • Sports
    Teams, athletes, matches, tournaments and sporting events.
  • Business
    Companies, markets, finance, trade and economic activity.
  • Sci/Tech
    Science, technology, software, research and innovation.

While completing the study

Please classify each article according to your own judgement. There is no need to search online or use external tools.

You will not be shown whether an answer is correct or incorrect while the study is running. This helps ensure that earlier questions do not influence your decisions on later articles.

About your responses

Your selections will be recorded for the purpose of evaluating the human–AI decision-making system. The study interface does not require you to provide your name or other directly identifying information.

Before you begin

Please complete the 10 articles in one sitting and choose the category that seems most appropriate, even when an article feels ambiguous.
