Why TF-IDF?

It gives more importance to informative words while reducing the influence of words that appear in almost every article.

Why Logistic Regression?

It is a strong, efficient text baseline and produces class probabilities that can later support deferral decisions.

Evaluation rule

The official test split is never used for training. This keeps the reported accuracy a fair measure of generalisation.
